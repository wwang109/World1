# Illustrated shop border atlas v2

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Runtime derivatives are twelve themed WebP frames encoded with the shop-borders group.

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one production-ready transparent fantasy game UI border sprite atlas, a precise 4-column by 3-row equal-cell grid, 12 hollow rectangular frame sprites, landscape canvas.
Primary request: more distinctive painted frames with moderately thick dimensional rims and large illustrated corner ornaments that reach inward over underlying shop artwork. Beautiful hand-painted fantasy craftsmanship, bright readable materials, crisp silhouettes, natural brush texture, clean alpha edges.
Composition: twelve separate front-facing rectangular frames, same outer footprint, all centered in their equal grid cells, consistent small transparent margins. Each frame has four substantial corner designs and simple continuous straight edge rails suitable for nine-slice stretching. Central 55 percent of every cell entirely empty transparent. Ornament concentrated within the outer 22 percent at the corners, edge rails within outer 8 percent. Keep center of top and bottom rails simple with no protruding badges. No overlap between grid cells.
Exact row-major theme order:
Row 1: steel SWORD with crossing blade tips and hilt guards in corners; hammered iron and bronze AXE with pronounced axe-head brackets; brass and steel LANCE with elegant spearhead corners; carved yew BOW with curved wooden limbs, bindings and bowstring accents.
Row 2: BEAST with ivory fangs, curved claws and leather bindings; FIRE with ember copper and vivid curling flame tongues; FROST with jagged translucent cyan ice crystal corners; LIGHTNING with golden metal and branching violet electric bolts.
Row 3: NATURE with twisting wood, leafy vines and bright moss; HOLY with ivory and gold sunburst corner rays; DARK with obsidian and violet crescent horns and amethyst; NEUTRAL with ornate gold, bronze and teal fantasy craftsmanship.
Depth: shaded bevels and painted highlights on the frame materials themselves. Every theme must have a visibly different corner silhouette, not merely a color swap. Corners are substantial and artful without sprawling over the empty center.
Constraints: actual alpha transparency both around every frame and inside every hollow center. This is a sprite atlas asset, not a game screenshot or a UI mockup. No background fill, no checkerboard illustration, no text, no labels, no numbers, no cards, no icons floating in centers, no watermarks. No scene art. All twelve themes exactly once in the specified positions.
