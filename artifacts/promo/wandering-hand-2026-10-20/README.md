# WANDERING HAND — playtest media pack

Finished local promotional package, ready for review. Nothing was uploaded or published.

- `trailers/`: two40-second H.264MP4 trailers,1920×1080 landscape and1080×1920 portrait,25fps, stereoAAC sound.
- `screenshots/selected/`: six curated nativePNG screenshots. `screenshots/all/`: all12 desktop/mobile counterparts. `screenshots/contact-sheet.png`: preview.
- `editable/timeline.json`: exact edit/copy/source offsets. `editable/render.mjs` and `audio.mjs`: rebuild scripts; run from the World1 checkout root with its installed dependencies. `editable/review.ts`: contact-sheet/cut-review renderer.
- `sources/`: original native gameplay recordings used in the edit. `provenance/`: capture scripts, saved states, shot manifests. `audio/`: original instrumental and documented editorial sound cues.

The trailer opens with actual moving normal combat and its real victory, then shows card selection, board management, route choices, a real map-hosted event, equipment and a region-boss preview. The boss preview does not claim a boss victory. The end card says JOIN THE PLAYTEST without inventing a link, date or availability claim.

All gameplay pixels come from the current local game. Separate representative runs are intercut; this is a montage, not one uninterrupted run. Equipment inventory was staged from existing catalog items; inactive set requirements remain honestly displayed. No fake gameplay, hidden HUD crop, AI cinematic footage or product-code edits were used.

Sound is an original quiet instrumental plus an editorial reconstruction using the game's clearedCC0 effects. Browser video itself was silent. No voiceover or third-party music. Credits and exact provenance accompany the files.

Quality checks: both fullMP4s decode successfully; both duration40.00s, square pixels and requested dimensions; stereoAAC48kHz. Measured integrated loudness−18.51LUFS, true peak−3.10dBTP, loudness range2.10LU. Every cut, title/end card and selected screenshot was visually reviewed. Captions sit outside the full game canvas. Native screenshots remain unaltered.
