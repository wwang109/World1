| 2026-08-31 | **Cast order: SETUP before the attack, AFTERMATH behind it** — ruled in two passes the same day. The first ruling was general, verbatim: *"I think gems or card affect should be clear when it happens and debuff usually should happen at the end so any attack always come first before applying their debuff effect if it applies one"*, and, on whether a DoT counts, *"like if attack cause bleed or poison the attack happens first then bleed or poison get applied"*. Applied to EVERY target-facing rider, that made a card's own stat debuff stop softening its own later hits, and the user then SPLIT the ruling, verbatim: *"maybe we should split the type of debuff like posion/bleed/burn are after attacks but stats debuff/buff are applied before the atk"*. Asked directly whether a card's own stat debuff should therefore amplify its OWN hit, **the user answered YES** — that self-amplification is the confirmed intent, not an accident of ordering. SO: **AFTERMATH** — `poison`/`burn`/`bleed`, plus the caster-side `lifesteal` sink that has trailed since 2026-08-26 — resolves behind every `damage`/`statStrike` of the cast. **SETUP** — `debuffStat`, `expose`, and every control/self-buff/guard/ward/heal line beside them (`stun`, `slow`, `burden`, `curse`, `disrupt`, `buffStat`, `guard`, `negate`, `ward`, `thorns`, `heal`, `shield`, `taunt`) — stays exactly where its card authored it, so a debuff written ahead of a hit is cashed in by that hit. `judgment_light@diamond` reads `hit 27 -DEF6 / MDEF -20% / hit 32 -DEF4` (21 then 28 on the MDEF-6 dummy); `ruinous_hex@diamond` deals 37, `armor_break@diamond` chops at `-DEF10` rather than `-DEF20` (21 on a neutral DEF-20 dummy), `hex_of_frailty@diamond` deals 28 — the numbers the user was shown and chose. `crippling_gore` is the card that carries BOTH classes and is the proof the split is real: authored `bleed, debuffStat, damage`, it resolves `debuffStat, damage, bleed`. **THE NORMALIZER ONLY DEFERS — IT NEVER HOISTS.** "Setup before the attack" is met by AUTHORING: the twelve cards that write a stat line AFTER their hit (`deep_freeze`, `glacial_spike`, `piercing_arrow`, `silencing_slash`, `slow_hex`, `crippling_strike`, `scorching_brand`, `rustbind_hex`, `rimebound_pact`, `hoarfrost_creed`, `marksmans_creed`, `stormrank_relay`, plus `hemorrhage` at Gold+) are left as authored, because hoisting them would be a strict power increase at an unchanged price — pricing, not ordering. OPEN FOR THE USER. GEMS: the same one normalizer runs over the gem-spliced kit, so a gem's DoT trails and a gem's HIT re-anchors the host's own DoT behind it; a gem's `debuffStat`/`expose` still splices at `post` (a gem is ADDITIVE to the host's kit, never hoisted ahead of it) — also open for the user. `pre` arms are untouched (`shieldBreak`, `comboBonus`/`chainBonus`, `exploit`/`stackBonus`/`taxBonus`/`desperation`, `shieldBurst`/`wardRelease`): behind the hit they would be DELETED, not delayed, and `second_bite` still cannot trigger the poison it applies. PL-NEUTRAL AND MEASURED: `actionsPriceDeci` sums per action and reads no sibling's position, so all 664 card x tier power levels and every gem instance price are byte-identical and nothing was re-solved. Six cards are reordered by the resolver (21 kits): `barbed_rampart`, `crippling_gore`, `gutting_cleave`, `hemorrhage`, `leeching_fang`, `sundering_roar` — their faces read in the same direction as the log | `castPhaseOf`/`orderCastRiders` + the `GEM_ACTION_PHASE` phase table (`src/engine/cards.ts`, folded into every return path of `resolveEffectiveSkill` — the resolver seam, never the loop); `tests/engine/castOrder.test.ts` |
